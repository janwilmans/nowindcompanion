# Privacy Policy — Nowind Companion

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

This privacy policy describes how the Android app **Nowind Companion** ("the app"), developed by Jan Wilmans ("I", "me"), handles your information.

## Summary

Nowind Companion does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The only network activity is downloading MSX content that you ask for.

## Information the app collects

**None.** The app does not:

- require an account or sign-in
- collect personal information such as your name, email address, location, or contacts
- use analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services
- show advertisements or include advertising SDKs
- send any data to me or to third parties

## Files on your device

The app can open files that **you choose** from your device's storage (for example, disk images or ROM files). These files are:

- accessed only when you select them, using Android's standard file picker
- read and used locally on your device only
- never uploaded, copied to a server, or shared with anyone

The app does not scan or access other files on your device.

## Downloading content from the internet

The app can download MSX content (such as disk images) from the internet so you can use it on your MSX computer. When it does, your device connects directly to the website hosting that content. As with any web request, that website can see your IP address and basic request details. The app does not send any personal information with these requests. Those websites are run by third parties, and their own privacy policies apply.

## USB connection

The app talks to a Nowind Interface cartridge connected over a USB OTG cable. This connection is used only to transfer data between your phone and your MSX computer. No information about the device or the connection is collected or sent anywhere.

## Third-party services

The app does not use any third-party services that collect information about you.

## Data sharing and selling

Because no data is collected, no data is shared with or sold to third parties.

## Data retention and deletion

The app does not keep any personal data. Any app settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same location with a new "Last updated" date.
